Frequently Asked

Questions, Answered

Everything jewellers usually ask before placing their first bulk order with us.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs vary by box type and customisation level — typically starting from 100–500 pieces for standard designs. Share your requirement on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the exact MOQ and slab pricing upfront.

Can you print or emboss our jewellery brand logo?

Yes. We offer foil stamping, embossing, debossing and full-colour printing of your logo, monogram or artwork in your brand colours. Send us your logo file to receive design mockups before production.

Where do you deliver, and what are the timelines?

We ship across India directly from our Hyderabad factory. Standard production takes 7–15 working days depending on quantity and customisation, plus transit time. Express options are available for urgent orders.

What materials and finishes do you work with?

Rigid paper board, leatherette, velvet, satin and foam inserts. Every interior is engineered to cradle delicate pieces — rings, necklaces, bangles and heavy bridal sets — without scratching.

Can we see samples before confirming a bulk order?

Absolutely. We can produce a pre-production sample of your custom design so you can check size, material and finish. Sample cost is adjusted against your bulk order confirmation.

Do you provide GST invoices for business purchases?

Yes — we are fully GST registered (GSTIN: 36AFMPN3258E1ZB) and issue proper tax invoices for every B2B order, suitable for your accounting and procurement records.

How do I get a quotation?

Use the enquiry form above or message us on WhatsApp at +91 93467 97060 with your box types, quantities and branding needs — you'll receive pricing, MOQ and delivery estimates, usually the same day.
